Overall Vision

Based on the letter "N," the logo uses diagonal lines and a gradient from dense to sparse to create a sense of "surface-to-deep" penetration. It directly echoes the product's core function — opening up skin channels and boosting serum absorption.

Product Color Redesign

产品颜色重塑

建立"暖调科技感"主色调
提升亲和力与专业温度。

主色采用哑光玫瑰金，配合底部细磨砂外壳，让金属不再冰冷，而是沉稳、有触感。
引入裸粉色作为辅助点缀，柔化产品与环境的对比，整体视觉从"实验室工具"转向"浴室里的安静伴侣"。

The primary color is a matte rose gold, paired with a fine matte finish on the shell — turning the metal from cold to grounded and tactile.
Nude pink is introduced as an accent to soften the contrast between the product and its environment, shifting the overall visual perception from a lab tool to a quiet companion in the bathroom.

The system creates a closed‑loop journey from infusion → nutrient delivery → post‑care recovery, addressing multi‑level skincare needs at home.

品牌虚拟模特

Product Matrix

via

通过品牌虚拟模特统一视觉输出
建立与用户的长期情感连接。

配套一次性针头
一次性，安全可靠

Via 是 NenoVia 的品牌虚拟形象，以统一的视觉语言贯穿所有传播触点。她不是超模，也不是网红，而是一位 28 岁、住在哥本哈根的护肤品配方师。雀斑、浅金短发、温和自然的穿搭，让她看起来既真实又亲近。通过 Via 的品牌声线与视觉输出，我们与用户建立长期的情感连接——不是冷冰冰的代言，而是像朋友一样陪伴每一次护肤仪式。

Via is the brand's virtual identity, unifying every brand touchpoint with a consistent visual language. She is not a supermodel or an influencer, but a 28‑year‑old skincare formulator living in Copenhagen. With freckles, ash‑blonde short hair, and a natural, understated wardrobe, she feels authentic and approachable. Through Via's voice and imagery, NenoVia builds an enduring emotional connection with users — not as a distant spokesperson, but as a friend who accompanies them through every skincare ritual.
